HOW MUCH IS ACTUALLY PUBLISHED
  Name, designation, department and email are published for all 280 faculty.
  Phone numbers exist ONLY for 40 of them - the heads of departments, the
  academic/administrative officers, and a handful of academic-council members.
  The college does not publish per-faculty phone numbers on the web directory,
  the profile pages, or the department sites. There is no further public source.

  Anything not shown below simply has no published number - it is NOT an omission
  in this compilation. No number here has been guessed, derived from an email
  address, or taken from a third-party site.

CONFIDENCE
  'High'    - Information Brochure 2026, the college's own current directory.
  'Medium'  - Academic Council / examination rosters (2023-26 tenure). These are
               older; the holder may have changed role or left. Marked in the entry.
  Where the two disagree, both numbers are shown rather than one being chosen.
